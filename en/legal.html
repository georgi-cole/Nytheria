Privacy notice

Innovation Creativity Opportunities Ltd operates the KoleQuant website and is the controller of personal data collected through the contact form. For privacy enquiries, contact kolequant@gmail.com or write to the registered office.

The form asks for your name, email, country, optional city and reason for getting in touch. You can also add a message. We use this information, with your consent, to reply or send the updates you choose to receive. We need your email address to contact you.

FormSubmit receives submissions and forwards them to the operator’s Gmail and ABV inboxes. Its documentation states that it keeps submission records for 30 days. Retention in the operator’s email records depends on the purpose of the contact, whether the correspondence has concluded and whether the subscription is active.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing kolequant@gmail.com. You can also request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where the relevant right applies. You can lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection.

Cookies and preferences

The site saves your chosen language in your browser’s local storage so it can use the same language on your next visit. You can clear it in your browser’s site data settings.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes.
