Professional approach

Different disciplines, one health profile

Medicine, genetics, nutrition, movement and laboratory science each play a distinct role in interpreting results.

What each discipline contributes↘How disciplines work together↘

What each discipline contributes

Clinical medicine

Connects findings with health history and medication, and identifies when medical follow-up is appropriate.

Medical genetics and counselling

Explains the scope of testing, inherited risk and implications for family members.

Nutrition and dietetics

Connects nutritional markers, health history and everyday habits with suitable dietary goals.

Exercise physiology

Considers physical capacity, activity and recovery when setting movement goals.

Health behaviour

Turns specific goals into habits that fit into daily life.

Laboratory quality

Considers methods, sample quality and testing conditions when comparing results.

How disciplines work together

  1. A shared baseline

    Each discipline starts with the same health history and set of results.

  2. A defined role

    Each professional interprets findings within their area of expertise.

  3. A coordinated review

    Priorities, follow-up and referrals are discussed together.
