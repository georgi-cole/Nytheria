What to expect

Operational questions deserve direct answers.

Where will samples be collected?

The Bulgarian collection route and laboratory partners have not yet been confirmed. They will be published before anyone is invited to pay or submit health information.

How long will results take?

Turnaround time depends on the final laboratory and biological-age/genetic methods. The service will publish realistic time ranges rather than promising instant science.

Will I need to prepare?

Preparation requirements will depend on the final panel and may include fasting, timing, medication guidance from the appropriate clinician, or avoiding intense exercise before selected tests.

How will results be delivered?

You receive a clear personal report together with guided interpretation and practical next steps.

What happens if something concerning appears?

The service must have an explicit escalation and referral pathway. Nytheria does not replace emergency, primary or specialist medical care.

How often will I retest?

There will not be one universal interval. Reassessment should depend on the marker, intervention, biological variability, clinical need and the question being tested.
