How Nytheria works

From your first measurement to follow-up

Six steps connect your health history and measurements with priorities and follow-up.

The six steps

Six steps in one health profile

Start with the questions that matter to you and track the measurements that can help answer them.

  1. Set your goals

    Discuss your health history, medication, day-to-day habits and past results with a professional.

  2. Take the measurements

    Testing establishes a baseline you can compare with later results.

  3. Bring the results together

    Laboratory, genetic and functional information is considered in one profile.

  4. Review with a professional

    Discuss what the results can tell you and which findings deserve a closer look.

  5. Set priorities

    Agree on specific next steps for discussion, action and monitoring.

  6. Track change

    Repeat relevant measurements and compare your new results with the baseline.

Before testing, during review and afterwards

Before a sample

Follow the instructions for the specific test from your laboratory or healthcare professional.

At your review

Bring previous results. Comparing them is most useful when the measurements were taken under similar conditions.

At follow-up

Discuss which markers to track and when to measure them again.
