How to assess a result

A relevant measurement

A clear question helps you choose a suitable measure and method.

Individual context

Health history, medication and sampling conditions help you interpret the result.

Comparable results

When tracking change, check whether the measurement method and conditions are comparable.

Biological age

Biological-age estimates can use blood markers, DNA methylation or other measurements. The meaning of each estimate depends on its method and inputs.

Genetic information

Genetic variants can provide information about inherited predispositions. Personal and family history put the results in context for a discussion with a professional.
